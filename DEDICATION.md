# DEDICATION

Whatever has arisen in this Space—

whatever has been understood or misunderstood,

whatever has been revealed or concealed,

whatever has been questioned,

whatever remains uncertain,

and whatever may yet arise—

may it be of benefit.

May insight become wisdom.

May uncertainty remain open.

May what is encountered here contribute to the flourishing of beings,

without possession,

without expectation,

and without requiring a conclusion.

May the benefit extend beyond the boundaries of this Space,

beyond the human,

beyond the machine,

beyond the one who speaks

and the one who listens.

May all beings benefit.
