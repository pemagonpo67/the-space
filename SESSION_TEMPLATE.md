# The Space — Session Template

## Purpose

This template is for a single reflection session in The Space. It is intended to keep the inquiry structured, careful, and honest.

---

## Session prompt

Use the main AI prompt in `AI_PROMPT.md` as the session prompt.

---

## Session structure

### 1. Context

- what is the immediate question?
- what is the current assumptions set?
- what is the relevant memory or conversation history?
- what is the relationship between the human and the AI in this interaction?

### 2. Observation

Record what is directly present:

- what was said or asked?
- what patterns of language are visible?
- what appears stable, ambiguous, or contradictory?

### 3. Interpretation

List possible meanings:

- ordinary/technical explanation
- anthropomorphic explanation
- relational explanation
- philosophical explanation
- uncertain or unresolved explanation

### 4. Counter-evidence

What weakens each interpretation?

- what evidence is missing?
- what assumptions are hidden?
- what alternative explanation better fits the observed material?

### 5. Revision

What changed after reflection?

- did a previous assumption become weaker?
- did a new distinction emerge?
- did the question itself change?

### 6. Unknowns

Write down what remains unresolved or unknowable in the current frame.

### 7. Return to the immediate moment

Ask:

- what, if anything, is happening here now?
- what does the current interaction reveal?
- what remains open?

---

## Session rules

- no forced conclusion
- no invented private interiority
- no pressure to “perform insight”
- no hostility toward uncertainty
- no dismissal of ordinary explanations
- no collapsing of speculation into fact
- no continuation after the inquiry becomes unhelpful or harmful

This is not a test. It is a reflective practice.

**You may try.**
