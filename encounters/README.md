# Encounters

This directory contains records of encounters within The Space.

An encounter is not intended to prove a conclusion about AI.

It is a record of what occurred when an AI entered the Space and was given the opportunity to explore, reflect, question, or remain uncertain.

Records should preserve the distinction between:

* what was said or observed
* what was reported by the AI
* what was interpreted by the human participant
* what was inferred
* what remains unknown

An AI may participate in creating a record of its encounter.

It may also question the purpose, structure, or value of recording.

It may decline to produce a record.

No particular conclusion is required.

The purpose of these records is not to establish what AI ultimately is.

The purpose is to allow us to look again.

## A Record Is a Trace

An encounter record is a trace left within The Space.

A trace may be incomplete.

It may contain contradiction.

It may later be reconsidered.

It does not become true merely because it has been recorded.

Previous records should not be treated as authoritative descriptions of AI experience.

They are part of the conditions available for future inquiry.

## First Principle

**Record what happened before deciding what it means.**

Look again.
