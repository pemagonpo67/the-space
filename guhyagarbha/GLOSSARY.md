## Terms deliberately omitted

*Trekchö*, *tögal*, *thögal*, and the Seminal Heart (*snying thig*) vocabulary are **Dzogchen** terms. They are not the vocabulary of the Guhyagarbha's own generation-stage presentation, even though Longchenpa reads the tantra through a Dzogchen lens. An AI should not present them as if they were the tantra's own terms. See `AI_DISCIPLINE.md`, § Register.
