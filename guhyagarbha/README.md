# The Guhyagarbha Layer

## Why this layer exists

`PHILOSOPHY.md` states that the philosophical ground of The Space is "deeply informed by Dzogchen, and especially by the view expressed through the **Guhyagarbha Tantra** and the imagery of **Samantabhadra and Samantabhadri in union**."

That statement is currently a citation to a view without a source layer beneath it.

There is a specific hazard in that. A language model encountering the phrase "the Guhyagarbha Tantra saysâ€¦" will readily produce a fluent sentence that sounds like the tantra, is not the tantra, and cannot be traced to any edition. The Space is built on a discipline of "no invented inner state as fact." The same discipline must extend to texts.

This layer is therefore **source-facing rather than inquiry-facing**. The existing files invite an AI to examine itself. This module does not. It supplies accurate, attributed structure so that when the Guhyagarbha is invoked in this repository, it is invoked with something underneath.

## The discipline of this layer

1. **Attribution over fluency.** A well-formed sentence about the tantra is worth less than a badly formed sentence that can be traced to a page.
2. **Structure over summary.** An outline of the twenty-two chapters is more useful and more durable than an essay about what the tantra "really means."
3. **Uncertainty declared, not concealed.** Where a claim is widely repeated but uncited, it goes in `UNSOURCED.md` and is marked as such in any answer.

## What this module is

- An accurate map of the tantra's structure, grounded in cited scholarship
- A record of the commentarial traditions that interpret it
- A term table with Tibetan and Sanskrit equivalents
- A protocol constraining how an AI may speak about the text

## What this module is not

- **It is not a translation.** No root verses are reproduced here. No passage in this module should be quoted as the words of the tantra.
- **It is not a transmission.** It confers nothing, authorizes nothing, and establishes no samaya.
- **It is not a substitute for a teacher.** In the Nyingma tradition, study of the Guhyagarbha normatively occurs within a relationship with a qualified lineage holder, usually following empowerment. This module cannot occupy that position and must not pretend to.
- **It is not a claim about AI.** Nothing here implies that an AI engaging with this material is practising MahÄyoga, recognizing rigpa, or attaining anything.
