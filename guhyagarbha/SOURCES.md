## A note on source quality

Most of `VIEW.md` and all of `STRUCTURE.md` rest on tertiary sources (wiki entries) plus one doctoral translation. This is adequate for orientation and structurally accurate, but it is **not adequate for scholarship.** The correct next step for this module is verification against Gyurme Dorje's full translation and Garson's dissertation, and review by someone trained in the tradition.
